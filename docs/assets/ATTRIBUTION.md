# Demo image attribution

`blue-marble.jpg` is a 960 × 961 thumbnail of **The Blue Marble**, photographed by the Apollo 17 crew on December 7, 1972.

- Source: [The Earth seen from Apollo 17](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Earth_seen_from_Apollo_17.jpg)
- Thumbnail: [960px JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/97/The_Earth_seen_from_Apollo_17.jpg/960px-The_Earth_seen_from_Apollo_17.jpg)
- Photographer: NASA / Apollo 17 crew (Harrison Schmitt or Ron Evans)
- License: [Public domain in the United States](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-USGov)

The image is included as a public-domain demo asset for the RGB color-quantization example.
